Evidence tiers

The headline counts documented attribution: an AuScope identifier or a written acknowledgement on the paper. Candidates are counted beside it until confirmed. Keyword matches and software use are listed, never claimed.

Documented attribution
The headline count. Verified, acknowledged-in-text and infrastructure-enabled combined: an AuScope identifier or a written acknowledgement on the paper itself. Candidates are counted beside this figure until confirmed, never inside it.
Verified
Identifier evidence. This paper carries AuScope's organisational identifier: the strongest machine-verifiable link, checkable by anyone from the paper's own metadata.
Candidate
Strong identifier evidence, awaiting confirmation. An author on this paper is a known AuScope-affiliated researcher at a partner institution. Strong evidence, but the organisational identifier is not yet on the record.
Acknowledged in text
Written acknowledgement. The paper's text explicitly acknowledges AuScope or one of its facilities.
Infrastructure-enabled
Used AuScope-supported infrastructure. The paper's text shows the research used AuScope-supported infrastructure, without naming AuScope in the acknowledgements.
AuScope software
Used AuScope-supported software. The paper reports using AuScope-supported software such as GPlates or Underworld. Counted separately from attributed publications, because the software is used far beyond AuScope.
Keyword only
No confirmed link yet. Found through an AuScope-related search term, with no identifier and no acknowledgement found. Under review, and excluded from every headline figure on this site.

Lenses and programs

The lens taxonomy is real (87 projects across six lenses) but lens is not a field on any dataset, publication or instrument record. It is inherited through 6 hand-written platform-to-project links, which reach 4 of 6 lenses, so lens totals are not published. Program groups come from this tracker’s search terms, not the Project Mapping sheet; never add a figure from one to a figure from the other.

Datasets, FAIR and infrastructure

Dataset counts are DOI-registered data products per platform. A record with DOI-registered children is a collection, listed but not counted; EarthBank sample records are counted separately. F-UJI scores use metric set v0.8, assessed 1 Nov 2026; a card shows the platform average. MT deployment figures come from AusMT run-level records, present for 31 of 161 surveys, so they are floors. Inventory figures (stations, instruments, boreholes) never feed the headline.

Glossary
Attributed publications
Documented attribution plus candidates. Papers carrying either an AuScope identifier or an explicit written acknowledgement, candidates included: verified, candidate, acknowledged-in-text and infrastructure-enabled combined. The hub headline counts only the documented three and shows candidates separately.
Unverified keyword matches
Excluded from the headline. Papers found by an AuScope-related keyword search with no confirmed link yet. They are listed separately, and excluded from every headline figure, so the exclusion is visible rather than assumed.
Citations
Times a paper has been cited, per OpenAlex. Counts come from OpenAlex and only ever increase in this tracker: a lower number from a later API call is treated as an API inconsistency, not as a correction.
Evidence grade
How firmly a paper is linked to AuScope. Every publication is graded by its strongest verifiable link: an identifier on the paper first, then an acknowledgement in the text, then a bare keyword match. Only the first two kinds are counted in headline figures.
Measured
Counted from records the tracker holds. A direct count of records in the tracker at the stated date. Click the number to see the rows it counts.
Snapshot
Frozen at the build date. Captured when this page was last built, not read live. The world may have moved since the stated date.
Estimate
Derived, not counted. Calculated or extrapolated from other figures rather than counted directly. The method is stated next to the number.
Partial
Some sources did not answer. This figure is real but incomplete: at least one source it depends on failed or was still loading. Treat it as a floor, not a total.
Unavailable
The source did not answer. The source behind this number failed or timed out, so no number is shown. This is missing data, not a zero.
Lens
AuScope's own grouping of its programs. The Downward-Looking Telescope frames AuScope's work as six lenses onto the continent. Lenses are a program taxonomy applied to projects; most of the site's evidence is not yet mapped to one, so lens counts describe coverage of the taxonomy, not the whole evidence base.
FAIR
Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable. Four principles for research data that a machine, not just a person, can find and use. FAIR is about how well a dataset is described and published, not about how good the science is.
F-UJI
An automated FAIR-assessment tool. F-UJI reads a dataset's public metadata and scores it against a fixed metric set. It measures the record, not the data: a superb dataset with a thin DataCite record scores poorly, and that is the point: the record is what a machine sees. Reference ↗
DOI
Digital Object Identifier. A permanent identifier for a published thing: a paper, a dataset, an instrument. Resolving it through doi.org should always reach the current home of that thing, even if the website behind it changes.
PIDINST
Persistent Identifier for Instruments. A metadata standard and DOI type for physical instruments, so a magnetometer or a seismometer can be identified and cited as precisely as a paper. AuScope mints instrument DOIs under the 10.82388 prefix. Reference ↗
