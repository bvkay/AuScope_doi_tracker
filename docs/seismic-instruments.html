Reading these numbers

  1. Coverage is the only defensible date type. Dates here are PIDInst Coverage, never Commissioned or DeCommissioned. StationXML InstallationDate, RemovalDate and CalibrationDate are null on every harvested row, and a back-test showed 18.6–21.3% of units that looked decommissioned at a historical cutoff were later redeployed — longest dormancy 20.3 years. A gap in a unit’s history is silence, not retirement.
  2. Channel epochs are data-acquisition epochs. They record when the archive received data from a channel, not when a technician installed or removed the box. Units still in service take the open-ended form YYYY-MM-DD/.
  3. Deployment-days are computed per station-epoch, channels deduplicated, and capped at today. A three-component sensor at one station for one epoch counts once, not three times, and a scheduled future end date can never inflate a total.
  4. An integrated node counts as one device. Phantom (built-in) dataloggers are dropped: a CMG-6TD, Certimus, Meridian or SmartSolo box is a single instrument, not a sensor plus a logger.
  5. AuSIS is excluded from the headline. The Australian Seismometers in Schools network is ANU-operated but permanently sited in schools — a separate collection with different deployment semantics, so it is not counted as campaign kit here.
  6. Serial cleaning is disclosed, not silent. Junk serials (999, 0000, unknown, bare digits) are excluded, SmartSolo spellings are collapsed onto the three real DTCC products by sensitivity, and truncated serials resolved from other networks’ full forms.
