Macquarie Ridge (3F 2020-2021)

How to cite Macquarie Ridge data ?
Reference: Hrvoje Tkalčić, Caroline Eakin, Nicholas Rawlinson, Coffin, M. F., & Joann Miriam Stock. (2020). Macquarie Ridge [Data set]. AusPass: The Australian Passive Seismic Server. Doi: 10.7914/SN/3F_2020


This network is comprised of 15 off-shore broadband ocean bottom seismometers (IGGCAS and AGOS) spanning October 2020 to November 2021 as well as 5 on-shore broadband stations (2020-2022) targeting Macquarie Island and its immediate surroundings.
